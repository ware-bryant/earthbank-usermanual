# Registration & Accounts

EarthBank is a bespoke, open data platform for the geochemistry community, developed by the AuScope Geochemistry Network (AGN) and partner Lithodat. It lets users visualise, synthesise, disseminate, and extract geochemistry data in a global geospatial context.

## Registering as a General User

Registering as a **General User** is quick: provide a name, email address, and password, and accept the EarthBank Terms of Use. General Users can explore, interrogate, and download all publicly visible data on EarthBank, and use the platform's built-in data visualisation and synthesis tools.

## Upgrading to Data Contributor

To upload data to EarthBank, a General User must first upgrade their account to **Data Contributor**. This requires providing additional information:

- Academic, government, or professional institution and role
- Institutional email address (if not already provided at General User registration)
- An [ORCID iD](https://orcid.org/register) (free to obtain if you don't already have one)

Data Contributors can upload, disseminate, and publicise data on EarthBank, and browse the data of their institution and its wider community.

## Managing your account

The account menu (top right of the **Map window**) is the access point for:

- Upgrading a General User account to Data Contributor, or checking account status and assigned institution
- Editing user information — first name, last name, email
- Changing your EarthBank login password
- Logging out of the platform

## First-time walkthrough

The first time you log in after registering, a pop-up in the **Map window** highlights featured data collections, useful tools, and short "how to" videos to help you get oriented. It reappears on subsequent logins unless you uncheck the box in its bottom-right corner. You can reopen it at any time from next to the EarthBank logo in the title bar.
