# How to Cite

Publications and reports that benefit from the use of EarthBank and its archived data should reference the following.

## General platform use

For general use of the platform, cite:

> Boone, S.C., Dalton, H., Prent, A., Kohlmann, F., Theile, M., Gréau, Y., Florin, G., Noble, W., Hodgekiss, S.A., Ware, B. and Phillips, D., 2022. AusGeochem: An Open Platform for Geochemical Data Preservation, Dissemination and Synthesis. *Geostandards and Geoanalytical Research*, 46(2), pp.245-259. doi: [10.1111/ggr.12419](https://doi.org/10.1111/ggr.12419). Open access.

## Thermochronology work

For work using thermochronology data (fission track, (U-Th)/He, and thermal history models) and associated analytical tools, also cite:

> Boone, S.C., Kohlmann, F., Noble, W., Theile, M., Beucher, R., Kohn, B., Glorie, S., Danišík, M., Zhou, R., McMillan, M. and Nixon, A., 2023. A geospatial platform for the tectonic interpretation of low-temperature thermochronology Big Data. *Scientific Reports*, 13(1), p.8581. doi: [10.1038/s41598-023-35776-3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-35776-3). Open access.

!!! note "To confirm with the team"
    Both papers were published under the "AusGeochem" name and should be cited exactly as published — titles and DOIs don't change with the rebrand. Worth double-checking whether a citation for "AuScope EarthBank" specifically (as opposed to AusGeochem) exists or is planned, since these two references predate the rebrand.
